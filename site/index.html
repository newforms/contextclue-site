A little context goes a long way.

Context Clue adds a small, customizable overlay to your video calls, sharing things like your local time, location, weather, mood, or how many coffees you’ve had.

A little more context. A little more connection.

Your context, your call.

Share as much or as little as you like. Choose what to show, where to show it, and how it looks.

Works with your favorite video apps.

Context Clue works as a virtual camera on your Mac. Just select it as your camera in Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, Teams, or any other compatible app.

Step away without disappearing.

Need a moment? Pause your video and replace it with a softly blurred still, while your context stays visible.

Free means free.

All features are free, with no trials, limits, or subscriptions. A small Context Clue watermark is the only difference.

Love it? Remove the watermark forever for $6.99.

Private by design.

Your video stays on your Mac. Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or shared with us. No analytics, no tracking.

Video processing happens entirely on your device, in a camera extension that can’t access the internet.

Location and weather come from Apple, using your approximate location. That’s it.

Made for Mac.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
